## Video 18 · Inheritance tax tail · 8:25–9:29 · Motion graphic

**[Script Section]** "But the bigger change for people leaving is inheritance tax. It used to depend on your domicile, which was famously hard to get rid of. Now it depends on whether you're a long-term resident, meaning you've been UK resident for at least 10 of the last 20 tax years. If you are, your worldwide assets fall within UK inheritance tax at 40%. And here's the part most people don't know. When you leave, that doesn't switch off straight away. There's a tail. If you've been resident for between 10 and 13 years, the tail is three years. For each extra year of residence, it gets longer, up to a maximum of ten years. So if you've lived here for 20 years and you move abroad, your worldwide estate could still face a 40% inheritance tax bill if you die within ten years of leaving.
