# 4. Visual Analysis + Visual Style Profile

- **Art style:** live-action talking head in a bright loft room, plus typographic motion cards (bold condensed uppercase, sage funnel "LEVEL" motif, numbered lists that build line by line), product cut-out graphics (object on pure white with a huge light-grey headline and a hand-drawn arrow), and source-document captures with orange highlights and a "SOURCE:" credit.
- **Color palette:**
  - Near-white #FAFAF8 and off-white #F4F4F2
  - Charcoal #1C1C1C for text
  - Mid-grey #9A9A9A for oversized headlines
  - Sage-to-forest green #3E5446 → #DCE5DE
  - Marker orange #F5A623
- **Lighting style:** soft diffused skylight daylight for live action. Flat lighting for graphics, with soft contact shadows on cut-outs.
- **Camera style:** static tripod shot at eye level. Graphics are flat and front-on. Documents are full-frame and straight-on.
- **Composition:** text on the left with a picture-in-picture inset on the right; objects centered with the headline behind; lots of empty space; captions bottom-left; a subscribe badge bottom-right.
- **Detail level:** low, so each frame makes one idea readable at a glance.
- **Mood:** calm, credible, editorial, evidence-led.

## Visual Style Profile (locked)

- **(A) Live-action B-roll:** bright Scandinavian-style UK home interior, white walls, wainscot panelling, sloped loft ceiling with skylight, natural wood and linen, soft diffused daylight, high-key exposure, static eye-level camera.
- **(B) Flat 2D typographic motion card:** #FAFAF8 background, charcoal bold condensed uppercase headings, numbered lists that build line by line, thin outline icons in grey circular badges, sage accents and stepped funnel, a hand-drawn black looping arrow, marker-orange highlights.
- **(C) Product cut-out graphic:** a photorealistic unbranded studio cut-out on pure white with a soft contact shadow, a huge light-grey condensed headline behind, and a thin hand-drawn arrow to a small uppercase label.
- **(D) Document capture:** a generic unbranded document shown full-frame and straight-on, with one key phrase highlighted in marker orange and a bold "SOURCE:" or "SMALL PRINT" credit.
- **Mood:** calm, credible, evidence-led, uncluttered.
- **Format:** 16:9.
- **Avoid:** real logos, real people's likenesses, neon, dark lighting, 3D gloss, clutter.
