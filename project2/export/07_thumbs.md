# 7. Thumbnail Analysis

- **Text style:**
  - Ultra-heavy rounded geometric sans-serif, all caps, 2–4 words.
  - A red box for the threat and a green box for the payoff.
  - White text with a soft shadow.
  - A handwritten spiral-pad sign variant with red circles.
  - Numbers as the hero.
- **Composition:** the presenter takes the left third to half, looking into the lens. Text and a single prop fill the right half. The background is a darkened loft room. Hands are used actively. Three elements at most.
- **Color contrast:** low-saturation charcoal, grey and white, with saturated red #E2231A and green #1FA84F. Red is kept away from the face.
- **Emotion triggers:** alarm, secrecy (the shush), contrarian surprise, transformation (before → after), loss aversion, authority.
